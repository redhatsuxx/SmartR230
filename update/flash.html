SmartR230 Firmware Updater

Flash new firmware to your SmartR230 BCM emulator directly from the browser.
Before you start:
  1. Ensure the SmartR230 Emulator is disconnected from the car's 12V power.
  2. This tool only works on a Windows computer (laptop or desktop). It will not work using a phone, iPad, etc.
  3. It will only work in Chrome or Edge. Other browsers (Firefox, Safari) are not supported.
  4. Connect the device via USB before clicking Connect, and do not unplug it or close this tab during flashing.
  5. Once the upload has completed, it will be safe to unplug and re-install.
