SmartR230 Firmware Updater

Flash new firmware to your SmartR230 BCM emulator directly from the browser.
Available firmware: v-
Before you start:
  1. Ensure the SmartR230 Emulator is disconnected from the car's 12V power.
  2. This tool only works on a Windows computer (laptop or desktop). It will not work using a phone, iPad, etc.
  3. It will only work in Chrome or Edge. Other browsers (Firefox, Safari) are not supported.
  4. Connect the device via USB to the computer.
  5. Click "Connect SmartR230" - a pop-up will appear, select "USB JTAG" and then click "Connect".
  6. Next, click "Flash Firmware".
  7. Do not unplug whilst the firmware is loading.
  8. Once the upload shows as completed (circa 10 seconds), it will be safe to unplug.
  9. Re-install into the R230. Good job!
It's now safe to remove the device.
